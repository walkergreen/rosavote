Cast → count → prove

Six steps from membership roll to a result anyone can check.

01

Import the roll

Upload your membership list once. RosaVote de-duplicates and issues a single-use code per member.

02

Send the codes

Codes go out by email or SMS through your provider of choice, with opt-out handled automatically.

03

Members rank or score

Voters open a clean, accessible ballot — rank a slate, score candidates, or answer a motion.

04

Close & count

At the deadline the ballots are tabulated by the binding method — Scottish/Meek STV, Score or STAR.

05

Publish the ballots

Anonymized ballots and a tamper-evident hash chain are published alongside the winners.

06

Anyone recounts

Members verify their receipt; observers re-run the count in independent software — same result, every time.

Verifiability, not vibes

Your ballot, provably in the count.

  • A receipt only you hold. Each ballot is hashed with its answers and a secret nonce — printed on your receipt, revealing nothing about how you voted.
  • A chain no one can forge. After close, every ballot links into a sorted hash chain. Change one vote and the published chain head no longer matches.
  • Checked in your browser. Paste your receipt into the in-page verifier — or run the bundled verify.py yourself. No trust in the operator required.
# your ballot receipt
receipt = 7F3A-2B9E-C4D1
record = sha256(receipt | answers | nonce)
a1c9…4f0b
# published chain (sorted by record hash)
chain[k-1] = 3e7d…9a2c
chain[k]   = sha256(prev | record)
= 6b8f…1d40

The preferred alternative

RosaVote is the better default.

OpaVote is a good partner to DSA — affordable, dependable, and a solid choice, especially for high-stakes or contested elections where an independent third party adds trust. RosaVote is the preferred alternative for everything else: stronger, more flexible, fully customizable, and more affordable. We're not forcing a migration — chapters choose what fits each race.

Different tools, different jobs

RosaVote and OpenSlides aren't rivals.

Some of DSA's most important decisions happen live, in a room. That's OpenSlides — and DSA uses it to run national convention and recommends it for live meeting votes. RosaVote handles the other half: asynchronous ballots members cast on their own time. The two barely overlap.

OpenSlides — the live assembly

Runs the room

The floor process itself: agenda, motions and amendments, speakers list, quorum, the projector, and live votes while delegates are present. Open source, and DSA's system for convention.

  • Motions, amendments & the whole floor process
  • Live Yes / No / Abstain and per-candidate votes
  • Real-time results, in the room, in the moment
RosaVote — the async ballot

Runs the election

Votes that happen before anyone's in the room: members ranking candidates on their phones over days, with proportional counts and results the whole membership can verify.

  • Scottish & Meek STV, Score/STAR — ranked, proportional elections
  • Single-use codes to 100,000+ members over days or weeks
  • Member-verifiable, independently recountable results

Ranked-choice STV elections aren't what OpenSlides is built for today — that's RosaVote's lane — and running a live convention floor isn't RosaVote's. DSA uses each for what it does best, and keeps recommending OpenSlides for live meeting votes.
